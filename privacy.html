Privacy

Privacy policy

Pension Planning is designed to keep your plan on your device.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Who is responsible for the app

Pension Planning is provided by Lee Donnellan. For privacy or support questions, email 66cognacs_roan@icloud.com.

Information you enter

The app can contain names, dates of birth, pension values, income, tax assumptions, expenses, retirement plans and other information that you choose to enter.

How information is handled

The current plan and app preferences are stored locally in the app's private container on your device. Pension Planning does not send this planning information to the developer, does not operate an account or cloud-sync service, and does not contain advertising, tracking or third-party analytics services.

CSV market-history files and JSON plan files are read only when you choose them. If you export or share a plan, the exported copy is controlled by you and by the destination you select using Apple's file or share interfaces.

Retention and deletion

Local plan data remains until you replace it, choose Start a new plan, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local app container from the device. You are responsible for deleting exported copies stored elsewhere.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store transactions, crash diagnostics or device information under Apple's own privacy terms. That processing is separate from the pension-planning information stored locally by this app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available on this page.
