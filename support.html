Support

How can I help?

For help, feedback or a problem with Pension Planning, contact the developer directly.

Email support

lee.donnellan@me.com

Please do not send a complete pension-plan export unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing its contents.

Helpful details to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The Pension Planning app version.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • The steps that reproduce the issue.
  • A screenshot with personal financial details removed where possible.

Before contacting support

Check the in-app Guide for explanations of plan inputs, tax assumptions, CSV import format, simulation percentiles and important modelling limits.
