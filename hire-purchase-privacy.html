Hire Purchase Calculator

Privacy policy

How the calculator handles your finance inputs, mileage figures and optional sharing.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who is responsible for the app?

The controller for the app is Lee Donnellan. For privacy questions or requests, contact 66cognacs_roan@icloud.com.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, postal address, phone number, financial-account details or precise location.

Finance amounts, dates, interest rates, balloon payments and mileage figures are processed on your device. The app does not automatically send those values to the developer or a remote analytics service.

The app does not include advertising, behavioural tracking, social-media features or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information kept on your device

Inputs are held in the app’s memory while the app is running. The current app does not write calculator inputs to a developer-controlled server or account. Use Clear all inputs to remove the current calculation from the app interface. iOS may retain data temporarily as part of its normal process and device-management behaviour.

Sharing and external links

If you choose Share estimate, iOS opens the system share sheet and sends the text you selected to the destination you choose. That destination’s privacy policy applies from that point onward.

The help section may open external websites, including MoneyHelper. Those websites are independent services with their own privacy notices. The app is not affiliated with a lender or MoneyHelper.

Website technical data

The privacy-policy website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider. Like most websites, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser type, time of request and security logs to deliver and protect the site. This processing is controlled by the host’s own terms and privacy policy. The policy page does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics scripts or marketing trackers.

Contact requests

If you email the developer, the contents of your message and your email address will be used to respond to you and deal with the request. They will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the correspondence, meet legal obligations and protect against disputes.

Legal basis and your rights

For contact requests, processing is normally necessary to respond to you or is based on legitimate interests in managing support and preventing misuse. Where consent is required, it will be requested separately.

Depending on your circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, restriction or objection to processing, and data portability. You can exercise these rights by emailing 66cognacs_roan@icloud.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

The app is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, website or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a material change is made.

Important limitation

This policy describes the current app design and intended website behaviour. It does not change the privacy policies of Apple, the hosting provider, email providers, lenders, dealers or linked websites.
