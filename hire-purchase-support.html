Hire Purchase Calculator

How can I help?

Support, feedback and troubleshooting for the Hire Purchase Calculator app.

Email support

For help with the calculator, feedback or feature requests, email lee.donnellan@me.com.

Please do not send bank details, lender account numbers, copies of finance agreements or other sensitive financial information.

Helpful details to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The Hire Purchase Calculator app version.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • The steps that reproduce the issue.
  • A screenshot with personal and financial details removed where possible.

Before contacting support

Check that you have entered the agreement’s figures correctly, including the APR, term, deposit, balloon payment and any mileage assumptions. The app provides estimates for planning and comparison; it is not a lender settlement quote or financial advice.

Privacy

Calculator inputs are processed on your device. If you email support, your message and email address will be used to respond to you. See the Hire Purchase Calculator privacy policy.
